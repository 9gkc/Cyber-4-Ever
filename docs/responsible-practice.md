# Responsible Practice

Cyber 4 Ever supports cybersecurity education, not unauthorized testing. The platform is deliberately static: it does not send requests to external targets, run scanning tools, gather target intelligence, or store learner data remotely.

| Situation | Expected action |
| --- | --- |
| You are using an intentionally vulnerable lab that you joined yourself. | Read the platform rules, remain inside that lab, and record only learning-focused observations. |
| You own the system or have written, current, specific authorization. | Follow the exact scope and constraints of the authorization. Stop if scope becomes unclear. |
| You found a potentially vulnerable public site, API, mobile app, or network. | Do not test it unless a published policy or direct authorization explicitly permits the exact activity. |
| You encounter credentials, personal data, tokens, or secrets. | Do not collect, reuse, store, or include them in your Cyber 4 Ever journal or Portfolio. |

> **The rule:** If you do not have explicit and specific authorization, do not test.

When a learning exercise reveals a safety concern, capture only the minimum information necessary within the platform’s authorized reporting process. Use the course, lab, or organization’s official reporting channel; do not turn a learning exercise into testing of an unrelated real system.
